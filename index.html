Sion Notes Uploader

시온의 원리노트 · Sion's Principle Notes

Sion's Principle Notes is a personal educational channel that explains the engineering principle behind a technology in the news in short 1–2 minute pixel-art videos.

Sion Notes Uploader is a private, single-user tool that runs on the channel owner's home server. Each episode's script is drafted with AI assistance, every factual claim is checked against primary sources, the animation is rendered by the owner's own code, and the owner personally reviews and approves every video before it is published.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's approved videos to the owner's own channel and to set their title and description. It has no other users, does not access any other channel or account, and is not offered as a service.
